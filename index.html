About JerriX Toys

Where play meets purpose

JerriX Toys is designed by IIT Guwahati alumni. We make India's first 3D chalk colouring kits: chalk models kids paint, display and then draw with, so one kit keeps giving.

Every kit is built around STEAM learning and screen-free play, using child-safe chalk (khadu) and non-toxic colours.
